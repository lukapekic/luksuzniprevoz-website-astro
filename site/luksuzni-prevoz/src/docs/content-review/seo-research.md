# Serbian-market competitor research — 11 September 2026

## Method and evidence limits

ValueSERP organic results were requested for Belgrade, Serbia, country `rs`,
desktop, Google `google.rs`, with Serbian, English and Russian query language.
Fourteen route intents × three locales were attempted; booking is deliberately
excluded as a noindex conversion step. The final query ledger records actual
successes and failures. A successful query returned 8–10 organic results in the
observed samples, despite requesting up to 20. These are dated samples, not
search volume, keyword difficulty, traffic, revenue, or stable ranking estimates.

Seven relevant local transport providers were selected from the returned organic
results, balancing general luxury transport, chauffeur hire and airport intent.
“Best” here means useful, visible comparators for this site's service mix; it is
not a claim about the quality of their transport. Own-domain results, directories,
marketplaces, self-drive rental and airport public-transit pages were excluded.
Grand Solutions and transferi.rs were excluded conservatively because of their
apparent relationship to the user's business; ownership was not independently
established in this task.

The repository's direct competitor collector received a verification interstitial
from every selected host. Those responses are excluded from technical findings:
missing title/description/canonical/schema in a challenge is **not** proof of a
competitor defect. Public search-crawler page extracts supplied the content and
heading comparison. For Transfers.rs and Transfers in Belgrade the inspected
content URL differs from the ranking URL and is recorded explicitly. Meta
 descriptions, raw canonical/hreflang and JSON-LD implementations remain unknown.
No traffic/backlink authority score or technical competitor score is invented.

## Search-intent findings

- The Serbian general query mixes chauffeur, kombi/minibus and directory results.
  The homepage must immediately say **prevoz sa vozačem** and explain its service
  range. Fleet owns model comparison; Pricing owns commercial terms.
- English chauffeur results favour service-plus-city language and duration-based
  travel. Airport results include public transit and marketplaces, so the airport
  page specifies a private vehicle, Nikola Tesla Airport and the arrival process.
- Russian `аренда авто с водителем Белград` returned many self-drive rental pages.
  The chosen homepage wording is an editorial refinement, not a measured higher-
  volume keyword. The private-driver page uses `личный водитель`, while airport
  copy uses `трансфер из аэропорта Белграда`.
- Corporate, delegation and conference queries produced some unrelated results
  and provider failures. Their keyword selections are lower-confidence intent
  hypotheses, supported by the actual service model and observed chauffeur
  competitors. They are not labelled proven demand. Keep the hub broad and its
  leaves specific: recurring company travel; delegation coordination; event
  arrival/hotel/venue schedules.
- Wedding and prom searches can imply stretch limousines, self-drive rental or
  party vehicles. Copy states a vehicle **with a chauffeur** and names actual
  models. No unsupported party amenities or body style is added.
