## Defaults proposed for approval

1. Use evidence-safe current-state wording; remove unsupported response times,
   performance endorsements, and staging/future promises.
2. Use `prevoz`, `usluga`, `Privatni vozač`, `aerodromski prevoz`, and `prevoz
   za posebne prilike` as the Serbian vocabulary.
3. Use polite-plural actions. Marketing booking entry is `Započnite
   rezervaciju`; actual submission remains `Pošaljite zahtev...`.
4. Use `raspoloživost`, `e-mail`, `e-mail adresa`, `(nije obavezno)`,
   `limuzina`, `kombi`, and canonical Mercedes-Benz model names. Do not use
   `vanovi` in Serbian customer copy.
5. Replace customer-facing `itinerer` with `raspored`, `raspored putovanja`, or
   a more precise phrase for the actual sequence.
6. Compress adjacent repetition while preserving every locked blueprint region
   and every important limitation.
7. Localize Serbian presentation of city/country and institutional names while
   preserving canonical data identities.
8. Keep legal/privacy claims narrower than the current unapproved form-retention
   posture.
9. Hold the published Business hub/navigation exposure until Corporate and
   Conference/Congress routes are activated, unless their locked contracts are
   explicitly revised.
