## Image role contract

Hero: `weeding-day-kissing.webp`  
Preserve couple + bouquet + visible vehicle context.

Story: `e-class-outside-weeding-day.webp`  
Contextual wedding transport; not fleet media.

Guest: `v-class-outisde-weeding-day.webp`  
Establishes additional group transport; no capacity overlay.

Presentation: `s-class-with-flowers-special-occasion.webp`  
Illustrative special request; adjacent copy must clarify decorations are not standard.
