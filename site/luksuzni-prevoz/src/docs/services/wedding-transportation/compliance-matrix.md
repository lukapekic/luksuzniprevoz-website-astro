## Component decisions

- The four `occasion-transportation` components are content-agnostic and contain no Wedding/Prom branching.
- The Wedding-day, guest-transport and presentation narratives remain Wedding-local because their content relationships and contextual imagery are page-specific.
- Existing Special Events components are not modified; their contracts and item cardinalities differ.
- Shared contextual imagery remains under `src/assets/shared/other/` because cross-page reuse is intentional.
- The established service-page SEO integration supplies FAQ structured data. No Event schema is introduced.

## Lifecycle gate

The localized entries remain `in-review` and `noindex: true`, and the route remains `scaffold`, until implementation and page verification pass. Publication changes all three lifecycle entries and the route availability together, followed by route, content, SEO, Astro, build and browser verification.
