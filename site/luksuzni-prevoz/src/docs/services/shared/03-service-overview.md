# Shared Contract — ServiceOverview

Status: **Shared structural contract**

## Purpose

Explain what the service is and what the standard service includes before deeper commercial or operational details.

## Default composition

Open dark section.

Desktop:

```text
5 / 7
heading + concise explanation | divider-led inclusion/fact rows
```

Tablet may retain two columns while readable. Mobile stacks copy first, facts second.

## Visual behavior

- no enclosing card by default;
- no image unless a page blueprint explicitly requires one;
- use typography and dividers before borders/shadows;
- compact inclusion rows rather than feature cards.

## Data/content

Facts come from verified data sources; editorial explanation comes from localized page content.
